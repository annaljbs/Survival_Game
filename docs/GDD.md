## 2. Learning goals

1. Don't rely on the phone without signal.
2. Water first, and treat unsafe water.
3. Shelter and warmth before night and rain; shelter before fire when everything is wet.
4. Budget time and energy; detours and shortcuts have costs.
5. Stay findable: signal at an open place; never eat unknown plants.
